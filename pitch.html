BATTING STRATEGY

  • Play with soft hands in powerplay to counter seam movement
  • Avoid driving on the up - wait for ball to come to you
  • Back-foot play recommended for first 6 overs
  • Target leg-side with sweep shots against spin
  • Build innings steadily - acceleration in middle overs
  • Be wary of variable bounce - keep shots along ground

BOWLING STRATEGY

  • Open with best seam bowlers - conditions highly favorable
  • Bowl good length consistently - avoid short balls
  • Use cross-seam deliveries for variable bounce
  • Spinners should flight the ball - grip will increase
  • Slower ball variations highly effective on this surface
  • Yorkers at death - wet pitch reduces effectiveness of length balls
