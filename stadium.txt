STADIUM OVERVIEW
================

Official Name: Eden Gardens
Alternative Names: Ranji Stadium (historically)
Location: B.B.D. Bagh, Kolkata, West Bengal, India
Established: 1864
Major Renovation: 2011 (for Cricket World Cup)
Total Capacity: 66,000 (post-renovation, reduced from 100,000)
Owner: Cricket Association of Bengal (CAB)

STADIUM CONFIGURATION
=====================

Structure Type: Oval/Horseshoe configuration
Orientation: North-South along long axis
Seating Tiers: Three main levels (Lower, Middle, Upper)
Roof Coverage: Approximately 58% of seating covered

5. CCTV coverage: 500+ cameras

- Large bags and backpacks
- Glass bottles and cans

- Laser pointers
- Banners larger than 1m x 1m

Arrival Recommendation:
- General admission: 3 hours before match
- Premium/VIP: 2 hours before match
- Media: 4 hours before match (accreditation)

SPECTATOR GUIDELINES (RAINY CONDITIONS)
---------------------------------------

Before Arrival:
1. Check match status on official website/app
2. Bring light rain jacket (ponchos available inside)
3. Waterproof footwear recommended
4. Keep electronic devices in waterproof covers
5. Arrive early to secure covered seating if available

During Match:
1. Follow stadium announcements regarding weather
2. Move to covered areas during heavy rain
3. Avoid metal structures during lightning
4. Do not stand on wet surfaces (slip hazard)
5. Keep tickets dry for verification

Weather Interruption Protocol:
1. Light rain: Match continues
2. Moderate rain: Covers on pitch, short delay
3. Heavy rain: Longer delay, spectators remain seated
4. Severe weather: Match suspended, evacuation if needed

DLS INFORMATION (For Spectators):
- If rain reduces play, Duckworth-Lewis-Stern applies
- Minimum overs for result: 5 overs per side
- Updated targets displayed on scoreboard
- Final result may be calculated mathematically

FACILITY ADJUSTMENTS FOR RAIN
-----------------------------

Food Courts:
- Additional covered seating deployed

- Dry snacks available

Restrooms:
- Slip mats placed at entrances
- Additional cleaning frequency
- Hand dryers activated

Walkways:
- Anti-slip mats on ramps
- Wet floor signs displayed
- Staff stationed to assist
